GTA6Plays Uploader – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

GTA6Plays Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the GTA6Plays accounts to upload their own short video clips to their own TikTok account.

What data the app uses. When the owner signs in with TikTok, the app receives an access token and basic profile information (display name and user ID) for the owner's own account. It uses these only to upload the owner's videos to that account as drafts.

Storage. The access token is stored only on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, shared or sent to any other party.

Other users. The app does not collect data from any other TikTok users or from viewers of the videos.

Deleting data. The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok's settings (Security → Manage app permissions), and delete the stored token from their computer.

Contact. Questions can be sent to the email address on the GTA6Plays account.
